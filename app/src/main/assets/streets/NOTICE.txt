Para selected road, railway, river and named commuter-landmark features,
rounded coordinates to seven decimals, and partitioned features by regional
ownership. A way crossing a regional border remains whole in its owner pack.
Rendering all 18 packs joins them on the same map without duplicate ways.
The archive stores zooms 8–14 with simplified tile geometry at lower zooms.
Coverage and completeness depend on the source OSM data. These packs are
not a routing engine, complete address database or authoritative boundary map.

The OSM-derived landmark records in place-search.sqlite are also available
under ODbL 1.0; GeoNames records retain their separate CC BY 4.0 attribution.
Reproducible transformations and the tile schema are
in tools/extract-philippine-streets.py and tools/build-regional-streets.mjs in
the Para source repository: https://github.com/d-Lucx/Para
The build guide documents source preparation and independent validation.

Local SDF glyphs derive from the bundled Inter font; see licenses/inter-OFL.txt.
GeoNames and regional boundaries have separate attribution in regions/NOTICE.txt.
