Para selects and clips pilot buildings, land use, water areas, walking paths
and classified roads. Polygon holes are retained. Entrances are associated
only with a named mapped landmark boundary; they are not verified vehicle
stops, and absent access tags do not establish public access.
This is geographic context, not a routing graph or complete address catalog.

Reproducible transformations and validation fixtures are in the Para repo:
https://github.com/d-Lucx/Para
tools/extract-pilot-detail.py
tools/build-pilot-detail.mjs
tools/verify-pilot-detail.mjs
tools/test-pilot-detail.py

The app retains independently attributed GeoNames/region data and local
font notices. No Organic Maps code, artwork, style file or binary map is used.
