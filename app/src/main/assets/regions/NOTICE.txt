Philippine regional overview maps

Regional boundaries: NAMRIA and Philippine Statistics Authority, via the
OCHA Philippines administrative boundaries (COD-AB) dataset, CC BY 3.0 IGO.
https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ab-phl
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/igo/
PSGC updates and source geometry: faeldon/philippines-psgc-shapefiles v1.0.0.
https://github.com/faeldon/philippines-psgc-shapefiles/releases/tag/v1.0.0
Para reprojected and simplified the regional boundaries together and split
the result into regional assets. These are approximate overview boundaries.

Place coordinates and names: GeoNames, CC BY 4.0.
https://www.geonames.org/
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Source: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/PH.zip
Para selected populated places and certain transport landmarks, assigned
them to the simplified regions, and generated an offline search index.
Coordinates for populated places represent a named point, not an address.

See tagum-davao-map-notice.txt for the separately bundled OSM street detail.
