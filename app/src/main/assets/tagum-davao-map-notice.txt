Source: OpenStreetMap API v0.6 bounding-box extracts.
The archive contains selected major roads, rivers, coastlines, and place labels
within 6.99–7.48 N and 125.51–125.89 E. Geometry is clipped to the pilot
boundary and coordinates are rounded to five decimal places. The data is
intended for destination selection and does not include every road or place.

Source extract and reproducible tiler: tools/tagum-davao-map.json and
tools/build-corridor-map.mjs in the Para source tree.
